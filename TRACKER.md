## How to use it

1. Create a repo called `frontend-journey` on your GitHub.
2. Click **Raw** on this file, copy everything below the line, and paste it into that repo's `README.md`.
3. Every week, tick the boxes and update your progress badge.
4. Share the repo link with your mentor. It's your public learning log.

> [!TIP]
> To update the badge, edit the number in its URL: `Progress-12%2F31` means 12 of 31 weeks. Change the color from `EF4444` (red) → `F59E0B` (amber) → `10B981` (green) as you go.
